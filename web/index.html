How to run the demo

  1. Open Live demo. On the phone, tap Medical SOS. The alert appears in the console.
  2. In the console press Accept alert, then On my way, Arrived, Resolved. The phone shows only these real steps.
  3. On the phone press Simulate crash. After the 20 second countdown an automatic Accident alert goes to a hospital and the police.
  4. Leave an alert alone for 20 seconds: it escalates to the next-nearest responder.
  5. Offline: under Demo tools tick "Pretend there is no signal", tap an emergency, watch the Morse flash, then untick to deliver it.
  6. Open 3D simulation for the crash, relay and help response.

Responders are sample data and dispatch is simulated. In a real emergency call 112.
